# 1.1.1 release record

Date: 2026-10-14. Version: 1.1.1. App build: 3. License: MIT.

This patch adds an administrator-managed local LaunchDaemon for use without an Apple Developer account, addresses helper registration from transient app locations, and provides explicit administrator-attended recovery for stale helper registrations. The repair restores and verifies the recorded sleep baseline before pinning the installed app signature. Failed recovery preserves the journal and prior trust.

Release notes come from CHANGELOG.md. CI and packaging must verify native Apple Silicon and Intel bundles, ZIP extraction, signatures, and checksums before publication under the ordinary immutable `v1.1.1` tag.

Administrative local installation, authenticated XPC activation, and real sleep-baseline restoration after normal disable, client exit, and lease expiry passed on the affected Mac; see VERIFICATION.md. Physical closed-lid operation and helper-crash/reboot acceptance remain pending. The builds are ad-hoc signed and not notarized.
